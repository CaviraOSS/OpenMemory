# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in LongMemory a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual and romantic identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, threats, insults, doxxing, and other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this code. Reports should be sent privately through the repository security contact or GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel. Maintainers will respect reporter privacy and apply proportionate corrective action.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
